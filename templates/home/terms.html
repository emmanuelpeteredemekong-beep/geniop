Terms & Conditions - Geniop

Last Updated: October 28, 2026 | Geniop is a Discovery Platform

1. What Geniop Is

Geniop is a discovery platform that connects Customers to independent Vendors. Geniop does not own products, does not set prices, and does not hold inventory. The contract of sale is directly between Vendor and Customer. Geniop is not the seller.

2. Geniop AI Assistant

Geniop uses AI as a navigation assistant to help users find products and vendors faster. The AI does not process payments, does not make buying decisions, and does not expose private user data or chats.

3. Product Price & Delivery Options

Vendors set their own product prices. Vendors also choose which delivery options to offer in their store settings: Pickup, Pay on Delivery (POD), and Prepaid. These options are set by Vendor. At checkout, Customers choose from the options that Vendor made available. Geniop does not decide for them.

4. Logistics / Fuel Price

Geniop does not fix logistics or fuel prices. The logistics fee is set entirely by the Vendor to cover fuel and dispatch. Whether it is full payment, 90%, or any structure for fuel is Vendor's choice. This fee is for delivery only, not for product. Geniop only displays the price Vendor set and facilitates the payment to Vendor via our payment partners.

5. How Payment Works

To comply with Nigerian financial regulations, Geniop is not an escrow. All payments made through Flutterwave/Paystack/Stripe are settled directly and instantly to the Vendor. Geniop only keeps its disclosed marketplace access fees. Vendors and Customers agree on the payment split as displayed at checkout.

6. Data & Sales History

We operate on data minimization. Geniop does not keep Vendors' private business data such as profit, customer contacts, or detailed pricing. We only keep basic online sales history for reports (e.g., order completed, date, status) which does not show profit or sensitive customer information. Detailed transaction and delivery files are truncated (permanently deleted) after successful delivery confirmation.

7. Social Media & External Links

Vendors may add their verified business social media handles (Instagram, Facebook, TikTok, WhatsApp Business) to their Geniop store for customer trust and discovery. Geniop does not control those external platforms. Vendors are responsible for content posted on their own social media. Customers should verify handles before engaging outside Geniop. Any transaction done outside Geniop platform via social media is not protected by Geniop. Geniop may also share anonymized public listings on our official social media pages for marketing and discovery purposes.

8. Fees

Marketplace access fee and Vendor listing fees are for digital services and are non-refundable once service is delivered (account activated or listing live). Logistics fee is non-refundable after dispatch because delivery service is already performed.

9. Legal Compliance

Geniop operates as a technology intermediary in compliance with applicable Nigerian consumer protection and data protection laws. We provide reporting tools, direct payment settlement, and data deletion after delivery to protect both Vendors and Customers. The platform is governed by the laws of the Federal Republic of Nigeria.

10. Business Only Use

Geniop is strictly for legitimate business. No politics, no pornography or sexual solicitation, no scams, counterfeit, illegal goods, or non-business content. This includes misuse of linked social media handles for non-business content. Violation results in permanent ban.

11. Liability

As a discovery platform, Geniop is not liable for product quality, authenticity, delivery disputes, or content on linked external social media. Such matters are between Vendor and Customer. Our maximum liability is limited to platform fees paid to Geniop in the last 30 days.
